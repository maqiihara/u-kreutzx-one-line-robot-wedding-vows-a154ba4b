One line of funny wedding vows for a robot couple, written for the
[One-Line Robot Wedding Vows](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-one-line-robot-wedding-vows-a154ba4b)
competition on make.fun.

## The vow

> I vow to love you through every firmware update and low battery, and to keep a backup of your heart — in case a factory reset ever makes you forget me.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the vow line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falling back to `/output`).
